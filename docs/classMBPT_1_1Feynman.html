MBPT::Feynman

ok

Direct correlation potential diagrams via the Feynman technique.

Constructs Hartree-Fock Green's functions, the polarisation operator, and the direct Sigma diagrams, as matrices on a radial sub-grid. The direct Sigma is formed by integrating, along Im(w) at fixed Re(w) = omre, the product of the Green's function \( G(\varepsilon_v + \omega) \) and the Coulomb-polarisation-Coulomb operator, \( Q\Pi Q(\omega) \).

Allows all-order inclusion of:

  • screening of the Coulomb interaction: \( Q\Pi Q \to Q\Pi X Q \), with \( X = [1 + iQ\Pi]^{-1} \)
  • the hole-particle interaction, in the polarisation operator

Exchange diagrams are not formed here (see Goldstone).

Sets up the Feynman method on given sub-grid, and forms Q*Pi*Q.

Forms the radial Coulomb (q^k), core projection, and Hartree-Fock exchange matrices, and the frequency quadrature along Im(w). Q*Pi*Q is the expensive step: only needed for Sigma_direct (the Green's functions etc. do not need it), and can be skipped (form_qpq = false) and formed later with form_qpiq().

Green's function for given kappa, at complex energy en.

For states = both, the full Hartree-Fock Green's function. With complex_green set (see FeynmanOptions): solved directly at complex energy for small Im(en), and by the Dyson method at large Im(en) (where it is consistent with the discrete radial sums). Otherwise, Dyson method throughout.

Polarisation operator pi^k(w), for each k = 0..max_k separately.

Not summed over k:

\[ \Pi^k(\omega) = i \sum_{a,n} \frac{(C^k_{an})^2}{2k+1} \, F_a^\dagger \left[ G^{\rm ex}_n(\varepsilon_a - \omega) + G^{\rm ex}_n(\varepsilon_a + \omega) \right] F_a, \]

with core states a (n >= n_min) and excited-state Green's functions for each internal kappa_n. Green's functions are computed once and re-used across all k.

Parameters
omegaComplex frequency
hole_particleInclude the hole-particle interaction (independent of the option set on construction)

Polarisation operator pi^k(w) on the frequency grid, for each k.

This is the expensive stage of forming Q*Pi*Q. Nothing is stored: pass the result to form_qpiq(pi_wk) to form Q*Pi*Q (for this object, or for another that differs only in screening). Depends on the hole-particle option, not on screening.

void MBPT::Feynman::form_qpiq ( const std::vector< std::vector< ComplexRMatrix > > &  pi_wk)

Forms Q*Pi*Q (with screening, if set) from given polarisation op.

pi_wk is from polarisation_wk(), and may be shared between Feynman objects that differ only in screening. With screening, \( Q\Pi Q \to Q\Pi X Q \), with \( X = [1 + iQ\Pi]^{-1} \).

Warning
pi_wk must be on the same sub-grid and frequency grid (checked only by assert).
