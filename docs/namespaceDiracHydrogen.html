Detailed Description

Exact relativistic hydrogen-like (Dirac-Coulomb) wavefunctions.

Analytic solutions of the single-particle Dirac equation for a pointlike nuclear charge Z (the Dirac-Coulomb problem). The orbitals have the form

\[ \psi_{n\kappa m}(\vb{r}) = \frac{1}{r} \begin{pmatrix} f_{n\kappa}(r)\,\Omega_{\kappa m}(\hat n) \\ i\,g_{n\kappa}(r)\,\Omega_{-\kappa,m}(\hat n) \end{pmatrix}, \]

with f the large (upper) and g the small (lower) radial component. Expressions follow H. A. Bethe and E. E. Salpeter, Quantum Mechanics of One- and Two-Electron Atoms (1977).

The optional lepton mass mass defaults to 1 (atomic units). The full relativistic energy is \( E = mass\,c^2 + \en_{n\kappa} = mass/\alpha^2 + \en_{n\kappa} \), and the radial functions scale with mass as \( f_{mass}(r) = \sqrt{mass}\,f_1(mass\,r) \).

Non-relativistic limit
Passing \( \alpha \le 0 \) selects the non-relativistic (Schrodinger) limit directly, rather than evaluating the Dirac expressions at a tiny \( \alpha \) (which is numerically unstable). In this limit the large component reduces to the non-relativistic radial function (f = P_nl), the small component vanishes (g = 0), and the energy is \( \en_n = -mass\,Z^2/(2n^2) \).
Note
Uses some numerically unstable functions (Gamma functions and confluent hypergeometric functions), so may be inaccurate for extreme inputs. For reasonable inputs (Zeff = 5, up to n ~ 10) tested good to at least parts in 10^12.

Single-particle energy, without rest mass: enk.

Bound-state Dirac-Coulomb energy, excluding the rest-mass term:

\[ \en_{n\kappa} = mass\,c^2\left[ \left(1 + \frac{(Z\alpha)^2}{(\gamma + n - |\kappa|)^2}\right)^{-1/2} - 1 \right], \]

evaluated in a numerically stable form (avoids cancellation between the \( O(c^2) \) terms). For \( \alpha \le 0 \) returns the non-relativistic energy \( \en_n = -mass\,Z^2/(2n^2) \).

Nonrelativistic bound-state radial function: P_nl(r) = r * R_nl(r)

Hydrogen-like Schrodinger radial function, unit normalised: \( \int_0^\infty P_{nl}^2\,\d r = 1 \). Mass scales as \( P_{nl,mass}(r) = \sqrt{mass}\,P_{nl,1}(mass\,r) \).

Evaluated via the confluent hypergeometric form \( \rho^l e^{-\rho/2}\,{}_1F_1(l+1-n, 2l+2, \rho) \), \( \rho = 2Z\,mass\,r/n \), so n may be non-integer (effective n). For integer n this reduces to the usual associated Laguerre polynomial.

Parameters
rRadial coordinate (atomic units)
nPrincipal quantum number; may be non-integer (effective n)
lOrbital angular momentum
zeffEffective nuclear charge, Z
massLepton mass, in units of the electron mass (default 1)
Returns
P_nl(r)
Warning
For non-integer n the series does not terminate and the function grows like \( e^{+Zr/n} \) at large r (not a normalisable bound state); it is only meaningful at finite r / near-integer n.
