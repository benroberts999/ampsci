15//------------------------------------------------------------------------------
16/*!
17 @brief Momentum-space (Fourier transformed) bound orbital, for the all-K
18 plane-wave response.
19
20 @details
21 The momentum-space orbital,
22 \f[
23 \tilde\psi_{am}(\vb{p}) = \int d^3r\, e^{-i\vb{p}\cdot\vb{r}}\,
24 \psi_{am}(\vb{r})
25 = (-i)^l \begin{pmatrix}
26 \tilde f_a(p)\, \ket{\kappa m} \\
27 -s_\kappa\, \tilde g_a(p)\, \ket{-\kappa, m}
28 \end{pmatrix}
29 \f]
30 (the spinors are functions of \f$ \hat{\vb{p}} \f$, and
31 \f$ s_\kappa = \kappa/|\kappa| \f$), has the radial functions
32 \f[
33 \begin{align}
34 \tilde f_a(p) &= 4\pi\int f_a(r)\, j_l(pr)\, r\, dr, \\
35 \tilde g_a(p) &= 4\pi\int g_a(r)\, j_{\tilde l}(pr)\, r\, dr,
36 \end{align}
37 \f]
38 with \f$ \tilde l = l(-\kappa) \f$, normalised as
39 \f[
40 \int (\tilde f_a^2 + \tilde g_a^2)\, p^2\, dp = (2\pi)^3.
41 \f]
42 The grid is logarithmic, and ends where the density
43 \f$ \tilde f_a^2 + \tilde g_a^2 \f$ has fallen below a set fraction of
44 its peak (see momentum_orbital()): the density is zero beyond p.back().
45*/
46struct MomentumOrbital {
47 //! Dirac quantum number of the orbital
48 int kappa{};
49 //! Binding energy (au); that of the real (HF) orbital
50 double en{};
51 //! Number of electrons in the orbital: (2j+1) times the occupation fraction
52 double num_electrons{};
53 //! Momentum grid (au), logarithmic
54 std::vector<double> p{};
55 //! Transformed large component, f~_a, at each p
56 std::vector<double> F{};
57 //! Transformed small component, g~_a, at each p
58 std::vector<double> G{};
59};

61//------------------------------------------------------------------------------
62/*!
63 @brief Fourier transforms a bound orbital onto a logarithmic momentum grid
64 (MomentumOrbital).
65
66 @details
67 The grid is logarithmic, @p points_per_decade points per factor of 10 in
68 p, running upwards from @p p_min, and stops (at most at @p p_max) once the density has fallen below
69 @p density_cut times its peak, beyond the peak. The radial integrals run
70 over the extent of the orbital (its max_pt()), with the cell-averaged
71 Bessel functions of SphericalBessel::fillBesselVec_kr, so the high-p tail
72 (from small r) is integrated correctly on a coarse grid.
73
74 @param Fa Bound orbital (as used in the matrix elements);
75 its occupation sets the electron count.
76 @param en Binding energy to record (au): that of the real
77 orbital, which may differ from Fa.en() for a
78 Zeff model state.
79 @param p_min First momentum (au).
80 @param p_max Largest momentum considered (au).
81 @param points_per_decade Grid points per factor of 10 in p.
82 @param density_cut Stop once density < density_cut times the peak.
83 @return The momentum-space orbital.
84*/
85MomentumOrbital momentum_orbital(const DiracSpinor &Fa, double en,
86 double p_min = 1.0e-3, double p_max = 1.0e4,
87 std::size_t points_per_decade = 150,
88 double density_cut = 1.0e-12);

90//------------------------------------------------------------------------------
91/*!
92 @brief Integrand of the all-K plane-wave response at bound-electron
93 momentum p: the Dirac traces of every factor.
94
95 @details
96 With z along q, \f$ \vb{p}_f = \vb{p} + \vb{q} \f$, and the angle between
97 p and q fixed by energy conservation,
98 \f[
99 \cos\theta_{pq} = \frac{p_f^2 - p^2 - q^2}{2pq},
100 \f]
101 returns the traces over the Dirac indices
102 \f[
103 T = {\rm Tr}\big[(E_f + c\,\vb{\alpha}\cdot\vb{p}_f + \beta mc^2)\,
104 \Gamma\,\rho\,\Gamma'^\dagger\big]
105 \f]
106 for the momentum-space density matrix of the shell without its
107 \f$ N_a/(8\pi) \f$ prefactor (2x2 blocks; see planewave_formFactors()),
108 \f[
109 \rho = \begin{pmatrix}
110 \tilde f_a^2 & s_\kappa\,\tilde f_a\tilde g_a\,\vb{\sigma}\cdot\hat{\vb{p}} \\
111 s_\kappa\,\tilde f_a\tilde g_a\,\vb{\sigma}\cdot\hat{\vb{p}} & \tilde g_a^2
112 \end{pmatrix},
113 \f]
114 where \f$ s_\kappa = \kappa/|\kappa| \f$. The traces are returned in the
115 order {V: 00, 33, 11+22, 03; A: 00, 33, 11+22, 03; Im VA 12; S; P}.
116 See planewave_formFactors() for the vertices and the use.
117
118 @param p Bound-electron momentum (au).
119 @param F Transformed large component, f~_a, at p.
120 @param G Transformed small component, g~_a, at p.
121 @param kappa Dirac quantum number of the orbital.
122 @param pf Ejected-electron momentum, p_f (au).
123 @param q Momentum transfer (au).
124 @param ef Ejected-electron kinetic energy (au).
125 @param alpha Fine-structure constant.
126 @return The 11 traces.
127*/
128std::array<double, 11> planewave_traces(double p, double F, double G, int kappa,
129 double pf, double q, double ef,
130 double alpha);

132//------------------------------------------------------------------------------
133/*!
134 @brief The 13 form factors of one orbital with a free (plane-wave)
135 ejected electron, summed over all multipoles, at energy transfer E and
136 momentum transfer q.
137
138 @details
139 The ejected electron is free (the potential does not act during the
140 collision), with kinetic energy, momentum, and total energy
141 \f[
142 \begin{align}
143 \en_f &= E + \en_a, \\
144 p_f &= \sqrt{\en_f(2 + \alpha^2\en_f)}, \\
145 E_f &= \en_f + mc^2,
146 \end{align}
147 \f]
148 and the bound electron has the momentum distribution of the orbital
149 (MomentumOrbital). The factors of the orbital are the Cartesian
150 components (z along q) of its response tensor,
151 \f[
152 R^{\mu\nu}_a = x_a \sum_{m_a}\sum_f J^\mu_{fa}\, J^{\nu *}_{fa},
153 \f]
154 summed over the final plane waves at energy \f$ \en_f \f$ and over the
155 closed shell. The sum reduces to a single integral over the
156 bound-electron momentum p, the angle between p and q being fixed by
157 energy conservation (planewave_traces()):
158 \f[
159 R^{\mu\nu}_a = \frac{1}{8\pi^2 c^2 q}\int_{|p_f-q|}^{p_f+q} p\,dp\;
160 {\rm Tr}\big[(E_f + c\,\vb{\alpha}\cdot\vb{p}_f + \beta mc^2)\,
161 \Gamma^\mu \rho_a(p)\, \Gamma^{\nu\dagger}\big],
162 \f]
163 with \f$ \vb{p}_f = \vb{p} + \vb{q} \f$, the trace over the Dirac
164 indices, and
165 \f[
166 \rho_a = \frac{N_a}{8\pi}\begin{pmatrix}
167 \tilde f_a^2 & s_\kappa\,\tilde f_a\tilde g_a\,\vb{\sigma}\cdot\hat{\vb{p}} \\
168 s_\kappa\,\tilde f_a\tilde g_a\,\vb{\sigma}\cdot\hat{\vb{p}} & \tilde g_a^2
169 \end{pmatrix}
170 \f]
171 the momentum-space density matrix of the shell (\f$ N_a \f$ electrons).
172 The vertex \f$ \Gamma^\mu = \gamma^0\gamma^\mu\tilde\gamma \f$ is that
173 of the operator:
174 - vector: \f$ (1, \vb{\alpha}) \f$
175 - axial: \f$ (\gamma^5, \vb{\Sigma}) \f$
176 - scalar: \f$ \beta \f$
177 - pseudoscalar: \f$ i\beta\gamma^5 \f$
178
179 The factors are the components
180 \f[
181 \begin{align}
182 V_T &= R^{00}, \\
183 V_L &= R^{33}, \\
184 V_E + V_M &= R^{11} + R^{22}, \\
185 X &= -{\rm Re}\,R^{03}, \\
186 A_T &= R^{00}_A, \\
187 A_L &= R^{33}_A, \\
188 A_E + A_M &= R^{11}_A + R^{22}_A, \\
189 Y &= {\rm Re}\,R^{03}_A, \\
190 Z &= 2\,{\rm Im}\,R^{12}_{VA}, \\
191 S &= R_{SS}, \\
192 P &= R_{PP},
193 \end{align}
194 \f]
195 where Z is the antisymmetric transverse vector-axial interference. With
196 the couplings in the vertex, \f$ \gamma^0\gamma^\mu(c_V - c_A\gamma^5) \f$,
197 the tensor is \f$ c_V^2 R_V + c_A^2 R_A - c_V c_A (R_{VA} + R_{AV}) \f$,
198 and its components are the coefficients of the spin-summed cross
199 section:
200 \f[
201 \begin{align}
202 R^{00} &= c_V^2 V_T + c_A^2 A_T, \\
203 R^{33} &= c_V^2 V_L + c_A^2 A_L, \\
204 R^{11} + R^{22} &= c_V^2 (V_E + V_M) + c_A^2 (A_E + A_M), \\
205 -{\rm Re}\,R^{03} &= c_V^2 X - c_A^2 Y, \\
206 -{\rm Im}\,R^{12} &= c_V c_A Z,
207 \end{align}
208 \f]
209 (for an isotropic target \f$ R^{21}_{VA} = -R^{12}_{VA} \f$, so the VA and
210 AV terms contribute \f$ {\rm Im}\,R^{12}_{VA} \f$ each), and
211 \f$ R = c_S^2 S + c_P^2 P \f$ for the scalar-pseudoscalar vertex
212 \f$ c_S\gamma^0 + i c_P\gamma^0\gamma^5 \f$. For an electron at rest the
213 factors reduce to the free-electron values, e.g.
214 \f[
215 \begin{align}
216 V_L &= (E/qc)^2\, V_T, \\
217 X &= -(E/qc)\, V_T, \\
218 A_L &= V_T.
219 \end{align}
220 \f]
221
222 The electric and magnetic multipoles are not separately defined by the
223 Cartesian tensor (only their sum enters a spin-summed cross section): the
224 whole transverse response is returned as the electric factor, and the
225 magnetic factors are zero.
226
227 @param orb Momentum-space orbital (momentum_orbital()).
228 @param E Energy transfer (au).
229 @param q Momentum transfer (au).
230 @param alpha Fine-structure constant.
231 @return The factors, in FormFactorSet order; all zero if the orbital is not
232 ionised (\f$ \en_f \le 0 \f$).
233*/
234std::array<double, 13> planewave_formFactors(const MomentumOrbital &orb,
235 double E, double q, double alpha);

237//------------------------------------------------------------------------------
238/*!
239 @brief Fraction of the norm of \f$ e^{i\vb{q}\cdot\vb{r}}\psi_a \f$ carried by
240 the multipoles K <= Kmax.
241
242 @details
243 \f[
244 c_a(q) = \sum_{K=0}^{K_{\rm max}} (2K+1)\int (f_a^2 + g_a^2)\,
245 j_K(qr)^2\, dr,
246 \f]
247 which tends to 1 by the identity
248 \f[
249 \sum_{K=0}^{\infty} (2K+1)\, j_K(x)^2 = 1.
250 \f]
251 The remainder 1 - c is the norm of the part of the state that the
252 multipoles above Kmax carry, and bounds what their omission can cost: the
253 plane-wave completion is skipped where it is negligible.
254
255 @param Fa Bound orbital.
256 @param Kmax Maximum multipolarity of the sum.
257 @param iq Momentum-transfer index in the Bessel table.
258 @param jK_tab Spherical Bessel table, on the orbital's grid.
259 @return The captured fraction.
260*/
261double captured_fraction(const DiracSpinor &Fa, int Kmax, std::size_t iq,
262 const SphericalBessel::JL_table &jK_tab);

264//------------------------------------------------------------------------------
265/*!
266 @brief The Bethe-ridge correction: completes the multipole sum of every core
267 orbital to all K with plane waves.
268
269 @details
270 The multipole sum truncated at Kmax misses the response near the
271 quasi-free (Bethe) ridge,
272 \f[
273 E \approx \frac{q^2}{2m},
274 \f]
275 where multipoles up to \f$ K \sim q\,r_a \f$ contribute. There the ejected
276 electron is fast and its high partial waves are undistorted, so the
277 missing part is supplied by plane waves. Per orbital and factor, the
278 returned correction is
279 \f[
280 \Delta K_a = K_a^{\rm PW}({\rm all}\ K) - K_a^{\rm PW}(K \le K_{\rm max}),
281 \f]
282 the all-K plane-wave response (planewave_formFactors()) minus the same
283 multipole sum evaluated with free Dirac spherical waves (DiracODE::freeDirac()), i.e.
284 exactly the plane-wave multipoles above Kmax. Added to the distorted-wave
285 factors of calculate_formFactors(), the total is
286 \f[
287 K = K^{\rm DW}(K \le K_{\rm max}) + \Delta K.
288 \f]
289 Off the ridge the
290 correction vanishes by itself; on the ridge the result is independent of
291 Kmax once the low multipoles, which carry the distortion, are included.
292
293 The plane-wave sum always runs K = 0..Kmax over the full continuum l
294 range, with the same operators, Bessel table, bound states, and
295 continuum truncation (max_pt) as the distorted-wave sum, so that the
296 K <= Kmax parts cancel to grid accuracy. It has no hole-particle or
297 orthogonalisation (distorted-wave physics only). Bound-bound (Pauli)
298 leakage of the plane waves is confined to K <= j_a + j_b and cancels as
299 long as Kmax >= 2 j_max of the core (checked). The squared factors are
300 clamped at zero (they are sums of squares; a negative value is noise from
301 the subtraction). The whole transverse correction goes into the electric
302 factors (see planewave_formFactors()).
303
304 The correction is computed only where it can matter: for each orbital,
305 at the momentum transfers where the multipoles above Kmax carry more than
306 @p ridge_eps of the norm (captured_fraction()), at the energies where
307 the orbital is ionised (ejected energy within ( @p ec_min, @p ec_max ),
308 and, at each energy, only where |p_f - q| lies within the momentum grid
309 of the orbital (elsewhere, far off the ridge, the plane-wave response is
310 zero by construction).
311 Parallel over the (orbital, E) pairs. Prints, per orbital, the momentum
312 transfer above which the correction is active.
313
314 @param vHF Hartree-Fock; its core defines the orbitals.
315 @param bound_states Bound state of each orbital as used in the matrix
316 elements (model_bound_states()); must be the same
317 as used for the distorted-wave factors.
318 @param ec_min Minimum ejected electron energy, in au.
319 @param ec_max Maximum ejected electron energy, in au.
320 @param Egrid Energy transfer grid, in au.
321 @param qgrid Momentum transfer grid, in au.
322 @param jK_tab Spherical Bessel table on @p qgrid (as used for the
323 distorted-wave factors).
324 @param Kmax Maximum multipolarity of the distorted-wave sum.
325 @param vectorQ Vector factors.
326 @param axialQ Axial-vector factors.
327 @param scalarQ Scalar factor.
328 @param pseudoscalarQ Pseudoscalar factor.
329 @param spatialQ Spatial (E, M, L) components.
330 @param ridge_eps Skip (orbital, q) where the multipoles above Kmax
331 carry less than this fraction of the norm.
332 @return The correction of each core orbital, indexed as the core, with the
333 allocation pattern of allocate_formFactors().
334
335 @warning Requires Kmax >= 2 j_max(core): if not, prints a warning and
336 returns a zero correction. Not defined for the diagonal (q = E/c)
337 case, which never approaches the ridge.
338*/
339std::vector<FormFactorSet> calculate_ridge_correction(
340 const HF::HartreeFock *vHF, const std::vector<DiracSpinor> &bound_states,
341 double ec_min, double ec_max, const std::vector<double> &Egrid,
342 const std::vector<double> &qgrid, const SphericalBessel::JL_table &jK_tab,
343 int Kmax, bool vectorQ, bool axialQ, bool scalarQ, bool pseudoscalarQ,
344 bool spatialQ, double ridge_eps);
