MBPT::Goldstone

ok

Second-order correlation potential, Sigma, from Goldstone diagrams.

Forms the second-order correlation potential for a valence state, as a non-local matrix on a radial sub-grid (every stride-th point of the full grid), by direct summation over a finite basis of internal core (hole) and excited states. Direct and exchange diagrams, with optional effective screening (fk) and hole-particle (etak) factors, and the two-body Breit correction.

Used by CorrelationPotential: for all diagrams (Goldstone method), or for the exchange and Breit diagrams only (Feynman method).

Splits basis into internal holes and excited states; forms Yk table.

Basis states found in core with n >= n_min_core are the internal holes; basis states not in core are the excited states. Calculates the Yk integrals for each excited-hole pair.

Parameters
basisBasis for internal lines (core and excited states); must not be empty
coreCore states: used to split basis into holes and excited states
i0Full-grid index of the first sub-grid point
strideSub-grid stride: every stride-th point of the full grid
sizeNumber of sub-grid points
n_min_coreMinimum n of core states included as internal holes (lower core states are frozen)
include_GInclude small-component (fg, gf, gg) parts of Sigma
BrBreit operator, for dSigma_Breit2 (copied; nullptr: none)

Direct diagrams, summed over internal holes and excited states, at energy en_v. Each multipolarity k is scaled by fk[k] * etak[k].

Calculate Exchange part of correlation potential.

fk are effective screening factors, applied to the multipole k of the outer Coulomb line; with fk_both_lines, also to the multipole of the inner line (inside \( P^k \)), i.e., \( f_k f_l \).

Calculates 2-body Breit correction to correlation potential.

Second-order diagrams with one Coulomb line replaced by the two-body Breit interaction (factor of 2: either line), including direct and exchange parts. Each multipolarity k is scaled by fk[k]; etak[k] scales the direct part only.

Parameters
kappa_vDirac quantum number of the valence state
en_vEnergy at which Sigma is formed
fkEffective screening factors, for each multipole k
etakEffective hole-particle factors, for each k (direct part only)
n_max_coreMaximum n of internal hole states (<= 0: all)
max_n_breitMaximum n of internal excited states (<= 0: all)
Returns
Breit correction to Sigma, on the sub-grid.
Note
Must have Breit: returns zero if no Breit operator was given to the constructor.
